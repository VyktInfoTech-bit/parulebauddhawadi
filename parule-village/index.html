About Parule Bauddhawadi

Nestled in the lush valleys of Konkan, Maharashtra, Parule is a village where ancient wisdom meets natural beauty.

Parule Buddhavihar, Rajapur, Maharashtra

A Village That Breathes Culture

Nestled in the lush Konkan region of Rajapur Taluka, Ratnagiri District, Parule is a peaceful village known for its natural beauty, strong community values, and rich Buddhist heritage. Home to 753 residents across 237 households, the village blends tradition, culture, and sustainable rural living.

Parule's greatest pride is its Bauddha Wadi, where, according to community history, the first Buddha Vihar in Rajapur Taluka was built entirely through Shramadan (voluntary community labour). This remarkable achievement reflects the village's spirit of unity, equality, and selfless service. Renovated and completed in 2015, the Buddha Vihar continues to serve as the cultural and spiritual heart of the community, inspired by the teachings of Lord Buddha and Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar.

SACRED HERITAGE

Takshashila Buddha Vihar

Nestled amidst the serene greenery of Parule, Tathagat Buddha Vihar stands as the spiritual and cultural heart of Parule Bauddhawadi. According to local community history, it is the first Buddha Vihar in Rajapur Talukabuilt entirely through Shramadan (voluntary community labour)—a remarkable achievement that reflects the unity, dedication, and collective spirit of the villagers.

Inspired by the teachings of Lord Gautama Buddha and the vision of Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar, the Vihar was later renovated and completed in 2015. Today, it continues to serve as a place for prayer, meditation, Dhamma discourses, community gatherings, and celebrations that strengthen the values of equality, compassion, and social harmony.

Buddha Vihar Journey

Buddha Vihar Timeline

A journey of unity, Shramadan, and unwavering faith spanning over six decades.

c. 1955

Shramadan Begins

Construction of the Buddha Vihar began through Shramadan (voluntary community labour). Families from Parule Bauddhawadi united to contribute their time, skills, and resources, laying the foundation for a place dedicated to Dhamma and equality.

1960

Buddha Vihar Completed

After five years of collective effort, the Buddha Vihar was completed. According to local community history, Parule became the first Bauddha Wadi in Rajapur Taluka to establish its own Buddha Vihar, becoming a symbol of unity, self-reliance, and the teachings of Lord Buddha.

2014

Renovation Begins

To preserve this historic landmark, the villagers initiated a complete renovation of the Buddha Vihar. Once again, the project was supported through the dedication and generous contributions of the community.

2015

Grand Reopening

The renovated Takshashila Buddha Vihar was inaugurated in 2015. The historic occasion coincided with the Rajapur Taluka Gat Kramank 3 Sayuktik Dr. Baba Saheb Ambedkar Jayanti, welcoming devotees and Buddhist communities from across the region.

Today

A Living Heritage

Today, Takshashila Buddha Vihar continues to serve as the spiritual and cultural heart of Parule Bauddhawadi. It hosts Buddha Jayanti celebrations, Dhamma discourses, meditation, educational activities, and community gatherings, inspiring future generations through the values of Peace, Equality, Compassion, and Fraternity.

Festivals & Events

Parule's festivals are moments of collective joy, cultural pride, and spiritual renewal.

May

Buddha Purnima

Celebrating the birth, enlightenment, and Mahaparinirvana of Gautama Buddha. The village gathers for prabhat pheri, prayers, and community feasts.

Major Festival
October 14

Dhamma Chakra Pravartan Din

Commemorating Dr. Ambedkar's historic conversion to Buddhism in 1956. A day of processions, Dhamma discourses, and cultural programs.

Community Day
April 14

Ambedkar Jayanti

The birthday of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar is celebrated with great reverence - felicitation programs, speeches, and cultural performances by village youth.

National Holiday
